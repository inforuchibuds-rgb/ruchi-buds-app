# Ruchi Buds app: Module 10 test checklist

Version 1.0 · 27-Nov-2026

Tick each line on the real app before signing off module 10. You need DS's login, the Accountant's login and a phone. Do it while the app is still in training mode, with the DEMO products, so no real stock or money is used.

**Sign-off gate:**

- A customer can order from the shop page on a phone, pay by UPI or cash on delivery, and see where the order is.
- Ordered packs are held at once, and the counter cannot sell them.
- Staff can check the payment, pack, dispatch into an invoice, record cash on delivery, and cancel.
- Only a Director or the FC can set up the shop.

## A. Set-up (as DS)

- [ ] The home screen has **Online shop orders**. It opens on **Orders**, with the 4 DEMO orders and the counts at the top.
- [ ] **Shop set-up**: enter the UPI ID and name, the pickup address and phone, tick Sales and Manager under Alerts, tick **Shop open**, Save. It says **Saved**.
- [ ] **Products online**: tap Fish pickle (DEMO) 200 g, add a photo from the phone, Save. The photo shows in the list.
- [ ] **Stock → Finished goods** shows **Total packs**, **On hold (online)** and **Free to sell**.

## B. Ordering on a phone (as a customer, not signed in)

- [ ] Open the shop link (Shop set-up → Copy the link) on a phone. The products show with photo, pack and price, and "Practice shop" at the top.
- [ ] Add 2 Fish pickle to the cart. **View cart**: enter a name, your mobile number, an address and PIN **682001**. It shows **Delivery to 682001: ₹40**.
- [ ] Try PIN **110001** after switching off the DEMO "\*" line: it says "we do not deliver to PIN code 110001". Switch the line back on.
- [ ] **Place order** (UPI). The order page shows the order number, **Please pay within 30:00**, the QR code and **Open my UPI app**.
- [ ] Do not pay. Type any 12 digits as the UPI reference and press **I have paid**. It says **we are checking your payment**.
- [ ] Order 1 Chilli powder 1 kg pouch for **pickup**, **Cash on delivery**. It says **Order received** and to pay in cash at pickup.
- [ ] Back on the shop page, Chilli powder 1 kg shows **SOLD OUT**.

## C. Staff (as DS)

- [ ] The home screen shows **Online shop:** with what is to do, and a count on the tile.
- [ ] Within 5 minutes, the Sales and Manager users get an email about the new orders (only once step 5 of the setup guide is done).
- [ ] At the counter (**Sales**), try to sell all the Fish pickle 200 g in stock. It refuses: some are **held for online orders**.
- [ ] Open the UPI order: **Payment found: mark Paid**. Then **Packed**. Then **Dispatched: make the invoice**. It says **Invoice … made**.
- [ ] **Invoices** shows the invoice for the customer, ₹640 with the ₹40 delivery charge, Paid.
- [ ] Open the cash-on-delivery order: **Handed over: make the invoice**, then **₹580 received**. The invoice shows as Paid.
- [ ] On the phone, the UPI order's page now says **Thank you!** with the invoice number.
- [ ] **Message on WhatsApp** on an order opens WhatsApp with the message ready (do not send it).
- [ ] Place one more order on the phone, then cancel it in the app with a reason. The packs are free again on the shop page.
- [ ] **Report**: the orders, value, UPI, cash on delivery and cancelled, by day and by product.

## D. Refusals

- [ ] As the Accountant: **Online shop orders** opens, orders can be handled, but **Shop set-up** says only a Director or the FC can change it.
- [ ] A dispatched order has no **Cancel order** button (use a sales return on the invoice).

## E. Before go-live

- [ ] Switch off or remove the DEMO delivery charges, add the real PIN codes, and put the real products on the shop.
- [ ] Ask your CA how online sales affect GST registration and invoices.
